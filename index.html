FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I need my own domain or email setup?

No. Emails go out from your own Gmail, which you connect once with one click. Replies come straight back to you.

Can I review emails before they go out?

Always. You see the email and can change anything, add links or remove people. Nothing is sent until you press Send.

Where do the leads come from?

From public business listings on maps and from each business’s own website. We show the email the business publishes, which is often a general address like info@ or hello@.

What do I pay for?

Leads. Your plan includes a number of leads with email each month, and only leads that come with an email count. Finding customer types and writing emails are unlimited. Top-ups add more leads whenever you need them.

Is cold email allowed?

B2B outreach is legal in most countries when it’s relevant, identifies the sender and offers an easy way to say no. Every email includes your business address and an opt-out line. Please respect anyone who asks you to stop.

Does it send emails by itself?

You press Send once and it emails every lead individually from your Gmail, up to 50 a day. Automatic follow-ups aren’t available yet.

What are hot leads?

Public posts on Reddit and X where people ask for what you sell. Our agent finds them, keeps only the ones ready to buy, and writes a reply for each. Turn on the weekly email and you get them every Monday.
